# 🦾 Iron Hero: Sky Assault

A fast-paced 3D endless flying shooter built with **React**, **Three.js**, and **React Three Fiber**. Take control of a futuristic armored hero as you soar through an infinite city, dodge skyscrapers, battle hostile drones, collect fuel, and survive for the highest score.

> **Note:** This game features an original futuristic armored hero and is **not affiliated with or based on any Marvel character or franchise**.

---

## ✨ Features

* 🌆 Infinite procedurally generated futuristic city
* 🦾 Smooth third-person flying mechanics
* 🔥 Fast-paced aerial combat
* 🎯 Multiple enemy types with increasing difficulty
* ⛽ Fuel management system
* 💥 Cinematic explosions and particle effects
* 🎵 Immersive background music and sound effects
* 🏆 High score system with local storage

## 🎮 Gameplay

Your mission is simple:

* Fly through the endless city.
* Avoid crashing into buildings.
* Destroy enemy drones and flying tanks.
* Collect fuel before it runs out.
* Survive as long as possible.
* Beat your highest score.

As time passes, enemies become stronger and more aggressive, making survival increasingly challenging.

## 📱 Controls

### Desktop

* **W / A / S / D** — Fly

## 🚀 Performance

The game is designed to deliver smooth gameplay across a wide range of devices.

Optimizations include:

* Low-poly environments
* Texture compression
* Object pooling
* Frustum culling
* Level of Detail (LOD)
* Adaptive graphics quality
* Efficient asset loading
